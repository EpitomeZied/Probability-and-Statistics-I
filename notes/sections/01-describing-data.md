# S01 — Describing data

> [!NOTE]
> Probability and Statistics I, section 01. Index: [Probability and Statistics I Sections](../section-plan.md) · questions: [Probability and Statistics I Question Bank](../../practice/question-bank.md) · teaching notes: [Probability and Statistics I Playbook](../teaching-playbook.md).

**One idea to land:** every summary statistic **discards information on purpose** — the skill is knowing which loss you can afford.

**Board:**
1. Two datasets with the same mean and wildly different spread. Then one with an outlier, and mean vs median moving apart. Salary data makes it land in one line.
2. Variance and standard deviation: why we square (and why SD is the one with usable units).
3. Sample vs population divisor (n−1 vs n) — say the reason plainly, do not hide behind "degrees of freedom."
4. Histogram vs boxplot on the same data: which question each answers.

**They solve:** compute mean, median, mode, variance, SD by hand for a small set; then add one outlier and recompute — which moved most, and why?

**Mistakes I expect:** using the population divisor for sample data; reporting variance in the data's units; median of an even-sized set; treating a bimodal dataset as having a meaningful mean.
