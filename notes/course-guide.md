## The subject in one line

The course is not a formula sheet. It is **"define the experiment, name the random variable, then choose the model"** — and every wrong answer in the term comes from skipping one of those three steps.

## Arc

Descriptive stats → counting → axioms → conditional probability and Bayes → discrete random variables → expectation and variance → named discrete distributions → continuous random variables and the normal → joint distributions → sampling distributions and the CLT → estimation and confidence intervals → hypothesis testing → review.
